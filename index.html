My working framework

Four stages. One learning loop.

Each stage creates better inputs for the next and a stronger basis for the next decision.

01 — DISCOVER

Research

Understand the product, customer, category, competitors, and constraints.

02 — DECIDE

Strategy

Turn evidence into an audience, offer, campaign, and measurement plan.

03 — DELIVER

Execution

Build cleanly, check tracking, launch deliberately, and capture a baseline.

04 — LEARN

Optimization

Read results against the hypothesis and feed learning back in.
